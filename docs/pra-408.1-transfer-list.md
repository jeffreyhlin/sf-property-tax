**Re: California Public Records Act request — Revenue & Taxation Code § 408.1 transfer list**

Dear Custodian of Records,

Under the California Public Records Act and Revenue & Taxation Code § 408.1, I
request an electronic copy (CSV, tab-delimited, or native export) of the public
list of property transfers your office is required to maintain, for all transfers
recorded in the preceding two years, including for each transfer the fields
enumerated in § 408.1:

- transferor name(s);
- transferee name(s);
- assessor's parcel number (block and lot);
- situs address of the property;
- date of transfer;
- date of recording and the recording reference (document number); and
- consideration paid, where known to the assessor.

Section 408.1 expressly declares this transfer information public and directs that
it be released. Section 408.1 also caps the inspection fee at the lesser of the
local agency's actual administrative cost or ten dollars ($10); I agree to pay that
fee. If your office maintains this list in electronic form, I request production in
electronic form under Government Code § 7922.570.

I understand that sales information taken from change-in-ownership statements is not
part of this list (§ 408.1(b)(7) and (f)); I am not requesting any confidential
change-in-ownership or preliminary change-in-ownership data.

Please confirm receipt and provide your determination within ten days
(Gov. Code § 7922.535). I am happy to receive this on a recurring quarterly basis
if that is simpler for your office; please let me know the process.

Sincerely,
Jeffrey Lin
[your email]

*Notes for Jeff (not part of the letter):*
- *This is the cheapest, cleanest path to NAMED, authoritative transfer data: it
  gives transferor/transferee names + APN + document reference + date, keyed to
  parcels, for $10, no scraping.*
- *"Builds up over time" plan: re-request quarterly. Each file covers the rolling
  last two years, so quarterly pulls accumulate a continuous named-transfer record
  going forward. Feed each file to `pipeline/deeds.py --input <file>`.*
- *What it does NOT give: the transfer TYPE (grant deed vs trust transfer) is not a
  §408.1 field, and neither is the exact sale price beyond "consideration where
  known." For deed document types you'd add the Recorder's grantor-grantee index
  (docs/pra-recorder-index.md). But §408.1 + our behavioral inference already
  upgrades confidence a lot, since matching surnames between transferor and
  transferee is a strong relational signal.*
- *Send from your email; subject like "Public Records Act request — R&T 408.1
  transfer list (electronic)."*

Attn: County Recorder / Custodian of Records
1 Dr. Carlton B. Goodlett Place, City Hall Room 190, San Francisco, CA 94102

**From:** Jeffrey Lin, [your email]

**Date:** [fill in send date]

**Re: California Public Records Act request (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.)**

Dear Custodian of Records,

Under the California Public Records Act, I request an electronic copy of the
official records index (the grantor-grantee index) maintained by the San Francisco
County Recorder for documents recorded from January 1, 2007 through the date this
request is processed.

For each indexed document, I request the index fields your office maintains,
including: document or instrument number, recording date, document type or title
(for example: grant deed, trust transfer deed, interspousal transfer deed, affidavit
of death of joint tenant), grantor name(s), grantee name(s), and the assessor's
parcel number where your index includes one.

To be clear about scope: I am requesting only the index, not copies or images of the
recorded documents themselves. The recorder's index is a public record required to be
maintained and open to inspection under Government Code sections 27230 et seq. and
27255-27257, and is subject to disclosure under the Public Records Act. Because the
index is maintained electronically, I request production in electronic format (CSV,
tab-delimited, or native export) under Government Code section 7922.570.

If any indexed field is confidential by statute, please redact that field and
produce the remainder rather than withholding records (Gov. Code § 7922.525(b)).

I agree to pay reasonable duplication or electronic-extract costs (Gov. Code
§ 7922.575, and any applicable recorder fee schedule adopted under Government Code
section 27366). If you anticipate fees exceeding $500, please send an estimate
before proceeding. If your office already offers a bulk index export or subscription
product for title companies or other regular purchasers, please treat this request
as an inquiry into purchasing that product and send the relevant terms and fee
schedule.

Please confirm receipt. I look forward to your determination within ten days under
Government Code section 7922.535.

Sincerely,
Jeffrey Lin
[your email]

*Notes for Jeff (not part of the letter):*
- *The index is the high-value target: document type + $0-transfer-tax patterns
  classify transfers, and grantor/grantee names confirm relationships. Title
  companies buy this data routinely, so a purchase path almost certainly exists.*
- *If they only offer the online search (recorder.sfgov.org) as the "access," the
  polite counter is that CPRA requires production of the underlying electronic
  records, not just a search interface.*
- *Expect them to exclude some protected instruments (e.g., records under the
  Safe at Home address confidentiality program); that's normal and fine.*

**Re: California Public Records Act request (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.)**

Dear Custodian of Records,

Under the California Public Records Act, I request copies of the following records
in electronic format (CSV, tab-delimited, or the native database export your office
maintains):

1. The current secured assessment roll for the City and County of San Francisco,
   including for each parcel: assessor's parcel number (block and lot), assessee
   name(s), assessee mailing address, situs address, assessed land value, assessed
   improvement value, applicable exemptions, use or property class code, and the
   date of the most recent change in ownership reflected on the roll.

2. The equivalent closed secured rolls for roll years 2007 through the most recent
   closed year, to the extent they are reasonably retrievable in electronic form.

The local assessment roll, including assessee names, is a public record open to
inspection under Revenue and Taxation Code sections 601 and 602 and Government Code
section 7922.525. Because your office maintains these records in electronic format,
I request production in electronic format under Government Code section 7922.570.

This request does not seek any information that is confidential by statute, such as
homeowners' exemption claimant information made confidential by Revenue and Taxation
Code section 408.2, or Preliminary Change of Ownership Reports (section 481). If any
portion of a responsive record is exempt, please redact that portion and produce the
remainder (Gov. Code § 7922.525(b)).

I agree to pay reasonable duplication costs, or the statutory cost of producing an
electronic extract where applicable (Gov. Code § 7922.575). If you anticipate total
fees will exceed $500, please contact me with an estimate before proceeding. If your
office sells the assessment roll as a standard data product, please treat this
request as an order for that product and send me the order form and fee schedule,
which may be the fastest path for both of us.

Please confirm receipt of this request. I look forward to your determination within
ten days as required by Government Code section 7922.535. I am happy to discuss
scope by email if it helps narrow or expedite production.

Sincerely,
Jeffrey Lin
[your email]
